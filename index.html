Where care breaks

Science is racing ahead.
Getting it to the patient is not.

Cancer care is full of small operational gaps with outsized consequences. The appointment moves. A prescription is never called in. Imaging is not booked. Records arrive after the consult. Everyone is involved, but nobody owns the space between.

The Orima model

AI explains the case.
Our team works it.

Orima connects the patient, family, a named human team, and specialized AI agents to one shared context—then carries each question across portals, phone calls, forms, scheduling, and follow-up until the loop is closed.

01

Orima Private Gateway

One living case context for notes, labs, imaging, genomics, insurance, decisions, and constraints.

02

Team Ori

A named human team owns the gaps, keeps the family oriented, and remains accountable for completion.

03

Ori Agents

Specialized agents extract decisions, prepare cited summaries, surface missing actions, and help execute the workflow.

From question to closure

Not another answer.
A completed loop.

Information is abundant. Accountability is scarce. Orima is being built to turn one question or missing step into a visible, verifiable sequence of work.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Ground the question in the complete case, not a single document.

  2. 02

    Decide

    Make the next action, owner, dependency, and deadline explicit.

  3. 03

    Execute

    Work across calls, messages, portals, scheduling, and paperwork.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Confirm the result, update the shared context, and close the loop.

The first wedge

Always-on trial matching for the whole patient.

A trial match is not a static search result. It changes as biology, treatment history, patient capacity, site availability, travel, and timing change. Orima is designed to re-evaluate the full picture and work the reachable options—not simply produce a list.

Private by architecture

The case should belong to the patient.

Orima is being designed around a dedicated case instance rather than a shared pool: private memory, case-specific context, and compute summoned for the work at hand.

Dedicated
One private workspace per case.
Contained
No shared memory or cross-case context.
Accountable
Every action and source stays connected to the case.
